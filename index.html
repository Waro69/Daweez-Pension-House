Common Questions

📍 Where is Daweez located?

We are centrally located in Tandag City, close to local shops and transportation hubs. Perfect for travelers exploring Surigao del Sur.

⏰ What are the Check-in times?

Check-in starts at 2:00 PM. Check-out is at 12:00 PM. Early check-in is subject to room availability.

🚗 Is there parking available?

Yes, we offer free, secure parking for our guests traveling with their own vehicles.

🍛 Is food available at the Pension House?

Yes! We serve delicious local meals as mentioned by our guests in their reviews.
